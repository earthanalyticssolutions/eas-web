01 · SCOPE

What does a good geotechnical investigation decide?

It decides how, on what and at what depth your project is founded. Without a solid investigation, the only certainty is that you are taking on risk silently. At EAS we combine real exploration with numerical analysis to deliver construction recommendations that are verifiable and defensible.

What we deliver

  • Allowable bearing capacity by level
  • Recommended foundation type and depth
  • Expected settlement (total and differential)
  • Site seismic analysis (soil profile type)
  • Water management, drainage, water table
  • Project-specific construction recommendations

What we DON'T do

  • "Desk" studies with no exploration
  • Generic, template-style recommendations
  • Signed reports without a site visit
  • Charging for tests that do not apply to the project
  • Data without traceability or chain of custody
  • Recommendations without numerical support
02 · GEOTECHNICAL EXPLORATION

We go into the subsurface. For real.

We design the exploration program around the project, the type of structure and the site conditions. Every meter drilled and every test pit has a technical purpose; nothing is done to fill a quota.

Manual and machine-dug test pits FIELD

  • Typical depth 1.5 to 4.0 m
  • Visual stratigraphic characterization
  • Disturbed and undisturbed sampling
  • Identification of fills and problematic materials
  • Photo log and technical description

SPT borings FIELD

  • Standard INV E-111 / ASTM D-1586
  • Depths of 6 to 30 m depending on the project
  • Blow count N for every 15 cm
  • Sample recovery for laboratory testing
  • Water table detection

Rotary core drilling FIELD

  • For rock masses or very competent soils
  • Core recovery for RQD
  • Discontinuity characterization
  • Extended depths as needed

Complementary in-situ tests FIELD

  • Dynamic cone (DPL / DPM)
  • Field density by sand cone
  • Permeability tests (Porchet, Lefranc)
  • Field vane shear in soft soils
  • Passive geophone for Vs30
03 · LABORATORY

Certified tests, not approximations.

Every sample recovered in the field enters the laboratory under chain of custody. Tests follow ASTM, INV-E or AASHTO standards as applicable, with calibrated equipment and certified technicians.

Physical characterization

  • Natural moisture content (INV E-122)
  • Grain size by sieve and hydrometer analysis
  • Atterberg limits (LL, PL, PI)
  • USCS and AASHTO classification
  • Specific gravity
  • Organic content

Compaction and strength

  • Standard and modified Proctor
  • Soaked CBR (1, 4, 7 days)
  • Field density by sand cone or nuclear gauge
  • Unconfined compression
  • Direct shear (CD, CU, UU)
  • Triaxial tests (CU with pore pressure)
04 · ANALYSIS AND DESIGN

From data to design.

With exploration and laboratory results in hand, we run the calculations. We do not copy templates or assume parameters: we use the project data and support it with recognized methodologies.

  1. Allowable bearing capacity Terzaghi, Meyerhof, Hansen and Vesić methods. Method selected according to soil type, foundation depth and water table. Safety factors stated.
  2. Total and differential settlement Elastic settlement (Boussinesq, Schmertmann), primary consolidation in fine-grained soils, and secondary consolidation where applicable. Checked against NSR-10 (Colombian Building Code) limits.
  3. Site seismic analysis Soil profile type per NSR-10 (A, B, C, D, E, F). Average shear wave velocity (Vs30). Aa, Av, Fa, Fv parameters and design spectrum.
  4. Foundation recommendation Type (shallow, deep, combined), embedment depth, minimum dimensions, construction joints, reinforcement, ground improvement if applicable.
  5. Water management and drainage Recommendations for water table control, perimeter drains, dewatering during excavation, foundation waterproofing.
  6. Slope and excavation stability Limit equilibrium stability analysis (Bishop, Janbu, Spencer) with GeoStudio or Slide. Recommendations for berms, drains and anchors.

06 · WORKFLOW

From quote to delivery.

You have clarity at every stage: what is being done, by whom, when and what comes next. No surprises, no "we're on it".

  1. Initial assessment · 24 to 48 h Site visit (if applicable), review of architectural drawings, understanding of the project. We define a preliminary program and deliver a detailed quote.
  2. Field work · 3 to 7 days Test pits, SPT borings, in-situ tests, photo log, sample collection under chain of custody. Daily progress report.
  3. Laboratory · 7 to 14 days Tests according to the approved program. Results with their equipment calibration certificates and the signature of the responsible engineer.
  4. Analysis and report · 5 to 7 days Calculations, modeling, interpretation, report writing, drawings, appendices. Internal peer review before delivery.
  5. Technical defense and support Digital and printed delivery. We defend the study before independent oversight, the permitting office (curaduría) or the agency. Post-delivery support during construction when criteria need clarifying.
