01 · SCOPE

Seeing the subsurface without opening it.

Geophysical exploration complements direct borings with a continuous image of the subsurface. Where a boring gives you a point, geophysics gives you a complete profile. For large projects, urban sites with access constraints, or to validate conventional exploration, geophysics is the best technical investment.

What we deliver

  • P- and S-wave velocity profile (Vp / Vs)
  • Thickness of subsurface units
  • Vs30 for NSR-10 (Colombian Building Code) seismic site class
  • 2D electrical resistivity tomography
  • Identification of faults, fractures and cavities
  • Detection of the water table and water bodies

When it is critical

  • NSR-10 requires measured Vs30 (not assumed)
  • Urban areas where drilling is not possible
  • Long linear road projects
  • Tunnels and underground works
  • Seismic microzonation studies
  • Detection of caverns and old fills
02 · SEISMIC METHODS

P-waves, S-waves, surface waves.

Different wave types reveal different properties. We combine methods for a complete and consistent characterization.

  1. Refraction seismic (P-wave) Layer thickness and compressional velocity. Standard ASTM D5777. Ideal for detecting top of rock, fill thickness and the water table.
  2. MASW (Multichannel Analysis of Surface Waves) Shear wave velocity Vs versus depth. For Vs30 and site seismic response. Standard ASTM D7400.
  3. ReMi (Refraction Microtremor) Vs in urban areas with ambient noise. Passive, no active source required. Useful where active MASW is not feasible.
  4. HVSR (Nakamura) Site natural frequency for seismic design. Identifies resonance and predominant periods.
  5. Crosshole / Downhole Vs and Vp measured in boreholes. Higher accuracy when boreholes are available. Standard ASTM D4428.
03 · ELECTRICAL METHODS

Resistivity as a clue to the subsurface.

The electrical resistivity of the subsurface changes with lithology, water content, salinity and the presence of contaminants. It is a powerful tool for mapping the subsurface in a non-invasive way.

Vertical electrical soundings (VES)

  • Schlumberger, Wenner, dipole-dipole
  • Variable depth of investigation
  • Identification of resistive vs conductive layers
  • Useful in hydrogeology and exploration

2D / 3D electrical resistivity tomography

  • Continuous cross-section image of the subsurface
  • Detection of contaminant plumes
  • Mapping of cavities and old fills
  • Aquifer characterization
  • Slope stability

05 · PROCESS

From the field to the subsurface model.

  1. Program design · 1 weekGeophysical lines set by objective: length, spacing, expected depth. Method selected for the problem at hand.
  2. Field acquisition · 3 to 7 daysDeployment of a 24-channel seismograph + active or passive source. Multi-electrode resistivity meter. Equipment calibration.
  3. Processing · 1 to 2 weeksFirst-arrival picking (refraction), dispersion analysis (MASW), 2D inversion (resistivity). Iteration until convergence.
  4. Interpretation · 1 weekConceptual model integrated with geology and conventional exploration. Vs30 calculation, unit identification, stratigraphic profile.
  5. Signed technical reportTechnical document with methodology, results, conclusions and recommendations. Appendices with raw data and open model files.
