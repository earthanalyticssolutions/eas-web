01 · SCOPE

The project's digital terrain.

We do not sell loose drawings: we deliver a complete digital model of the project. Centimeter-level orthophoto, digital terrain model, contour lines, slope maps, property base map, GIS layers. All georeferenced to MAGNA-SIRGAS (Colombia's official geodetic datum) and reproducible in QGIS or ArcGIS.

What we deliver

  • Georeferenced orthophoto (2-5 cm accuracy)
  • Digital Terrain Model (DTM)
  • Digital Surface Model (DSM)
  • Contour lines at project-specific intervals
  • Slope and aspect maps
  • GIS layers (points, lines, polygons)
  • QGIS / ArcGIS geodatabase delivered

When it is critical

  • Lot purchase or sale (actual area, boundary)
  • Urban or road design
  • Environmental studies and POMCA
  • Construction staking
  • Property inventories
  • Watershed analysis
02 · FIELD DATA CAPTURE

GNSS RTK + drone + total station.

We combine methods according to site conditions and the required accuracy.

GNSS RTK ±1.5 cm

  • Dual-frequency RTK base + rover receivers
  • Tied to IGAC's official MAGNA-SIRGAS reference system
  • Geodetic control points
  • Construction staking and verification
  • Ideal in open areas with clear sky view

RPAS drone photogrammetry

  • Flights with drones authorized by the Colombian Civil Aviation Authority
  • High-resolution orthophoto (GSD 2-5 cm)
  • Hectares covered in hours, not days
  • DSM and DTM by image matching
  • Ground control points (GCP)

Total station

  • For areas with dense vegetation or under cover
  • Staking with millimeter accuracy
  • Survey of existing structures
  • Tunnels and underground works

Bathymetry

  • Single-beam echo sounding for rivers and reservoirs
  • Survey of channels and cross sections
  • Input for hydrology and hydraulics
  • Volume computation for dams and reservoirs
03 · DIGITAL PRODUCTS

What is delivered for the project.

  1. Georeferenced orthophotoDistortion-free, scaled aerial image in the official reference system. 2-5 cm accuracy. Base for urban design, planimetry and visual communication.
  2. Digital Terrain Model (DTM)Bare-earth surface, without vegetation or buildings. For hydraulic, road and earthworks studies.
  3. Digital Surface Model (DSM)Includes vegetation and buildings. Useful for visibility, shading and volume analysis.
  4. Contour linesGenerated from the DTM at an interval suited to the project (0.5m, 1m, 5m). Smoothed and editable.
  5. Thematic mapsSlope, aspect, hydrography, drainage, critical zones. Each with professional symbology and legend.

05 · PROCESS

From planning to digital delivery.

  1. Planning · 24hScope definition, area to cover, required accuracy. Flight permits if applicable. Quote with schedule.
  2. Field work · 1-5 daysSetting control points, drone flight, GNSS RTK measurements. Photo documentation.
  3. Processing · 1-2 weeksPhotogrammetric processing, orthophoto generation, DTM/DSM, georeferencing, product quality control.
  4. GIS analysis · 1 weekLayer generation, spatial analysis, thematic maps, layouts. Building the deliverable geodatabase.
  5. Report + deliveryTechnical document with methodology, achieved accuracies and products. Printed + digital delivery of all files.
