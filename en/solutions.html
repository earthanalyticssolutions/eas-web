[ THREE DEPTHS OF THE SAME INVESTIGATION ]

Three levels. One analysis.

These are not three different services; they are three depths of the same geotechnical investigation. Each level adds more information about the ground to the design and reduces uncertainty. You choose how far to go based on the project and the decision you are going to make.

01 ▸ BASIC LEVEL USE · PRELIMINARY SIZING

Basic exploration

The first reading of the subsurface. A basic exploration of the ground with soil classification and estimation of strength parameters. Enough for preliminary sizing, checking feasibility or making a preliminary decision.

WHAT IT INCLUDES

  • Basic geotechnical exploration of the ground (test pits or preliminary borings)
  • Identification of layers and general stratigraphy of the site
  • Basic characterization: USCS / AASHTO classification, grain size, Atterberg limits, natural moisture content
  • Estimation of strength parameters through correlations and code tables
  • Preliminary recommendations for preliminary sizing
WHAT IT IS FOR

Preliminary sizing of foundations, checking the feasibility of a lot, guiding a preliminary decision or deciding whether it is worth taking the investigation further.

WHAT IT DOES NOT INCLUDE

No advanced characterization of the ground: it does not include strength tests or field measurements. Useful to get started, not to close the design.

02 ▸ INTERMEDIATE LEVEL USE · CONSTRUCTIBLE DESIGN

Intermediate exploration

The investigation goes deeper. Soil strength tests are added, now with parameters measured on site (not correlations). Geotechnical modeling with the project's real data. Enough to support a construction design.

WHAT IT ADDS (on top of basic)

  • Exploration with greater density and depth (SPT borings, multiple test pits)
  • Soil strength tests: direct shear, unconfined compression
  • Geotechnical modeling with parameters measured on site
  • Bearing capacity, settlement and stability analysis
  • Preliminary sizing by zone + basic drawings and typical details
  • Preliminary quantities + reference cost estimate for the works
WHAT IT IS FOR

Supporting the construction design with robust, buildable engineering. Ready for permit review, third-party oversight and the start of construction.

WHAT IT DOES NOT INCLUDE

No in-situ parameter measurement or advanced numerical modeling. Keeps safety margins above the optimum.

★ FULL SCOPE
03 ▸ DETAILED LEVEL USE · FINAL DESIGN + CONSTRUCTION

Detailed exploration

The full investigation. Detailed exploration of the ground with parameters measured directly in the field, special laboratory tests and advanced numerical modeling. Final design and detailed construction cost estimates, ready to build.

WHAT IT ADDS (on top of intermediate)

  • Parameters measured directly in the field (in situ): permeability, pressuremeter, dilatometer, geophysics
  • Special laboratory tests: consolidation, triaxial, residual strength
  • Advanced numerical modeling with finite elements (PLAXIS / MIDAS)
  • Final geotechnical and structural design (foundations, retaining walls, slopes, drainage)
  • Integrated drainage system (flow + discharge + critical zones)
  • Detailed cost estimates with final quantities of work
  • Complete construction drawings with reinforcement and specifications
  • Technical support during construction (site visits)
WHAT IT IS FOR

Making investment decisions based on precise data. Reducing uncertainty to a minimum and optimizing the real cost of the works. For projects where oversizing or redesign costs more than the investigation.

WHAT IT BRINGS

Precise optimization of materials and sections. The initial investment in fees is offset by direct savings during construction.
