[ STANDARDS AND BACKING ]

We work to code. We comply before we deliver.

Every investigation complies with Colombian regulations and the applicable international standards. Our reports are ready for permit review, third-party oversight and public agencies.

NSR-10

Colombian Seismic-Resistant Building Code

Title A (seismic design) · Title H (geotechnical investigations) · Title C (concrete). Calculations signed under the responsibility of a geotechnical engineer.

INVIAS

General construction specifications

INV E test methods and specifications for roads and pavements (INVIAS, Colombian National Roads Institute). CBR, Proctor, grain size and structural design per the INVIAS manual.

IGAC

Official mapping and georeferencing

Topographic surveys georeferenced to MAGNA-SIRGAS. Map products compatible with the cadastre and the land-use plan (POT).

IDEAM

Hydrological and climate data

Complementary studies use official IDEAM data for rainfall, runoff and regional climate characterization.

SGC

Colombian Geological Survey

Official geological mapping, formations, lithological units, faults and geodynamic processes for applied geology studies.

NTC / ASTM

Testing standards

Test procedures under Colombian technical standards (NTC) and ASTM standards for soils, aggregates and concrete.

[ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ]

What clients ask before hiring us.

If your question is not here, message us on WhatsApp or fill out the form. We reply in less than 24 business hours.

How long does a geotechnical investigation take? +
For a house or small project: 7 to 12 business days from the initial visit. For commercial buildings or subdivisions: 15 to 25 days depending on the number of borings and tests required. You receive the initial quote in less than 24h after your request.
Can the report be submitted for permit review / third-party oversight? +
Yes. All our technical reports comply with NSR-10 and include a statement of responsibility signed by the geotechnical engineer in charge. They are valid for the municipal permit office (curaduría urbana), oversight consultants (interventoría), municipalities and public agencies.
Do you work in my area? +
We operate across Latin America from our base in Colombia (Norte de Santander). We coordinate fieldwork on site wherever it is needed, while analytics, modeling and deliverables are handled remotely. For projects outside the country, we evaluate based on scope and the mobilization required.
What does a basic soil investigation include? +
Geotechnical exploration (test pits or borings depending on the lot), basic laboratory tests (moisture content, grain size, Atterberg limits, USCS classification), bearing capacity analysis, recommendations on foundation type and embedment depth, and a signed technical report.
Do you charge for the quick site visit? +
Yes. The quick site visit (PKG-01) has a nominal fee that is credited if you hire the full investigation afterwards. It includes the site visit, photo log, preliminary recommendations and a formal quote.
Can you support construction after the report? +
Yes. Package PKG-06 (Construction quality control) includes field density tests, compaction control, laboratory testing, periodic reports and corrective recommendations. We also provide on-call support by the technical hour for builders who already have their own team.
How many borings or test pits are done in each investigation? +
It depends on the lot area, the type of project and the complexity of the ground. NSR-10 sets minimums based on built area and building type. As a general rule, small house: 1-2 test pits. Mid-size house or warehouse: 3-5 test pits or borings. Commercial building: 4-8 SPT borings. Subdivision: we define the exploration grid based on the topography.
Do you take on small projects or only large ones? +
We work on any size. We have specific packages for single-family homes (PKG-02), mid-size commercial projects (PKG-03) and tailored large projects. If your project is small, we will tell you: sometimes a site visit with recommendations is enough and you don't need a full investigation.
Do you offer surveying and drone work as a standalone service? +
Yes. GNSS RTK surveying, drone orthophotos, DTMs, contour lines and slope maps can be hired on their own by architects, builders or owners who already have their geotechnical investigation but need map products.
How do you invoice and what payment methods do you accept? +
We invoice as a registered company meeting all Colombian legal requirements. We accept bank transfers, deposits to savings or checking accounts, and electronic payments. For mid-size and large projects we work with a 50% advance at the start and the remaining 50% upon delivery of the final report.
