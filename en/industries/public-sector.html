01 · SCOPE

For those who manage territory or deliver public works.

Municipal governments, departmental governments, national agencies (ANI, INVIAS, ANLA, SGC), Regional Autonomous Corporations (CAR), water sector institutions (IDEAM). The subsurface and the territory are a public responsibility, and EAS provides auditable technical evidence.

Who we serve

  • Municipalities and departmental governments
  • ANI · INVIAS · ANSV (roads)
  • ANLA · MADS · CAR (environment)
  • SGC · IDEAM (risk · water)
  • IGAC · cadastral agencies
  • Public utilities (water utilities, EPM, etc.)
  • EPC contractors on public works

Project types

  • POT · POMCA · EOT · review
  • Municipal seismic microzonation
  • EIA · PMA · DAA
  • Geotechnical studies for public works
  • Diagnosis of existing works
  • Hazard and risk maps
  • Technical support for tenders / contracting
02 · TYPICAL PROBLEMS

What clients in this industry bring to us.

  1. "We need to update the POT/EOT"Base mapping, hazard/risk layers, microzonation, GIS integration. Technical support throughout the review process.
  2. "We have a project going to tender"Tender documents require technical studies: geotechnical, environmental, hydrological. Documentation fit for the notice to proceed and final acceptance.
  3. "A rural district was cut off by a landslide"Emergency geotechnical diagnosis, immediate and medium-term solutions, support for securing funds from UNGRD (national disaster risk management unit).
  4. "We need seismic microzonation"Vs30 study + response profiles + seismic response maps. For the POT and NSR-10 (Colombian Building Code).
  5. "The oversight consultant is asking for more studies"Technical reinforcement during execution, supplementary testing, technical opinions that hold up under audit.
03 · HOW WE SOLVE THEM

Three scopes. The size of the project defines which one.

Rural district · minor works INITIAL

  • Basic geotechnical/hydraulic study
  • Signed report fit for audit
  • Timeline 2-4 weeks

Mid-size municipality INTERMEDIATE

  • POT · POMCA · territorial diagnosis
  • GIS mapping · thematic layers
  • Timeline 2-3 months

Departmental · national ADVANCED

  • Comprehensive studies by component
  • Numerical modeling · extensive campaigns
  • Ongoing support to independent oversight

Emergency · UNGRD RAPID

  • Site visit 48-72 h after the event
  • Immediate technical diagnosis
  • Short- and medium-term solutions

05 · PROJECT TYPES WE COVER

From a rural district to a departmental land-use plan.

  1. Minor public works (rural district, street)Geotechnical and hydraulic studies, surveying. Documentation fit for small-value direct contracting.
  2. Municipal POT / EOTBase mapping, integration with risk, hydrology and environment. Technical support during the review process.
  3. POMCA · river basinComprehensive basin studies, hydrology, risk, environment. A core document for water management.
  4. Seismic microzonationVs30 measured on an urban grid, response profiles, spectra, maps for NSR-10.
  5. EIA · PMA for infrastructurePhysical component (geology, soils, hydrology) coordinated with the biotic and social components.
  6. Diagnosis of existing worksTechnical audit of public works in operation: roads, bridges, slopes, buildings.
  7. Post-disaster emergencyRapid diagnosis, immediate solutions, support for securing UNGRD/CAR funds.
06 · ANY PROJECT SIZE

A rural district or an entire department. Same rigor.

In the public sector, signed technical responsibility is a legal requirement. A small municipality with a tight budget needs the same technical quality as a national agency; only the contractable scope changes.

If it is small

  • Documentation fit for direct contracting
  • Timelines aligned with the public schedule
  • Signed report backed by professional liability
  • Proportional cost

If it is large

  • Coordinated interdisciplinary team
  • Documentation fit for public tenders
  • Ongoing support to independent oversight
  • Traceability for audits · comptroller
  • Support during execution
