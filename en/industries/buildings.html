02 · TYPICAL PROBLEMS

What clients in this industry bring to us.

Every project comes with its own scenario. The most frequent:

  1. "I am about to build and need the soil investigation"The most common. Before the building permit or the start of excavation, you need subsurface characterization, bearing capacity and signed foundation recommendations under NSR-10 (Colombian Building Code).
  2. "My building has cracks or settlement"Active or recent distress. Forensic diagnosis to identify the root cause: differential settlement, expansive soil, change of use, overloading, lack of maintenance.
  3. "I was asked for a second technical opinion"The lot buyer, a bank, an insurer or the permitting office. We validate the existing study or issue an independent opinion.
  4. "I want to expand / add another floor"Capacity check of the existing foundation under the new loads. Coordination with the structural engineer.
  5. "I am buying a lot. Is it suitable for building?"Geotechnical feasibility before purchase: suitability for construction, risks, approximate foundation cost based on the soil.
03 · HOW WE SOLVE THEM

Three scopes. You choose the depth.

Every EAS proposal comes with three options: Initial, Intermediate and Advanced. Your project type, your budget and the risks you want to cover determine which one you select.

For a small house INITIAL

  • Test pits + basic lab tests
  • Bearing capacity
  • Foundation recommendation
  • Signed report · 2 weeks

For a commercial / mid-size building INTERMEDIATE

  • SPT + full lab testing
  • Vs30 seismic analysis
  • Foundation design + settlement
  • Structural engineer coordination · 4 weeks

For a high-rise / institutional building ADVANCED

  • SPT campaign + geophysics + full lab
  • Advanced numerical modeling
  • Site-specific seismic microzonation
  • On-site support · 6+ weeks

For a building in distress FORENSIC

  • Detailed inspection · monitoring
  • Root-cause hypotheses
  • Defensible remediation plan
  • Support for technical arbitration

05 · PROJECT TYPES WE COVER

From 60 m² to 30 stories.

Same technical variables, different depth. The type of building defines the scope you need, not whether we take on the project.

  1. Single-family homeA 1 to 3 story house. Test pits + basic lab tests + foundation recommendation. Timeline 2-3 weeks. Suited to the Initial or Intermediate scope.
  2. Mid-rise multifamily buildings3 to 8 stories. SPT + seismic analysis + foundation coordinated with the structural engineer. Intermediate or Advanced scope.
  3. High-rise buildingsOver 8 stories. Extended SPT campaign + measured Vs30 + deep foundations + numerical modeling. Advanced scope.
  4. Commercial · offices · shopping centersVariable loads, underground parking, site drainage. Comprehensive study with an emphasis on soil-structure interaction.
  5. Hospitals · institutional · educationalSpecial seismic category (NSR-10 K1, K2). Site microzonation. Foundations with a higher factor of safety.
  6. Hotels · tourism · recreationOften a mix of heavy zones (kitchens, lobby) and light zones (guest rooms). Foundations differentiated by zone.
