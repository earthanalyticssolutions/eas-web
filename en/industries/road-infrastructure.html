01 · SCOPE

For those who design, build or run road concessions.

Road concessionaires, INVIAS or ANI (National Infrastructure Agency) contractors, municipalities with tertiary roads, private contractors building access roads to developments, mining companies with internal roads. You design or build roads. EAS makes the subsurface credible.

Who we serve

02 · TYPICAL PROBLEMS

What clients in this industry bring to us.

  1. "I need to design the pavement structure"Subgrade CBR, traffic counts, AASHTO or INVIAS design, drawings, quantities, budget and technical specifications.
  2. "My road keeps getting potholes"Repaving is not always the answer. Distress survey, cause identification (subgrade, drainage, materials), design of the right rehabilitation.
  3. "I am going to build a bridge"Geotechnical investigation for pier and abutment foundations, channel hydraulics, scour analysis, construction recommendations.
  4. "I need pipe culverts / box culverts"Catchment hydrology, crossing hydraulics, sizing, support for tendering or contract award.
  5. "There is a moving slope next to the road"Geological-geotechnical study, slope stability modeling, design of retaining structures, drainage and monitoring.
03 · HOW WE SOLVE THEM

The scope adapts to the scale of the road.

Rural road · placa huella INITIAL

  • Test pits + quick CBR
  • Design per the INVIAS tertiary roads manual
  • 2-week timeline · low budget

Urban / secondary road INTERMEDIATE

  • Characterization by homogeneous segments
  • AASHTO design + drainage
  • Basic drawings + unit price analysis

Road concession / major corridor ADVANCED

  • SPT campaign + geophysics
  • Numerical modeling · stability
  • Final design + construction support

Bridges and minor structures SPECIALIZED

  • Integrated geotechnical + hydraulic study
  • HEC-18 scour analysis
  • Pier and abutment foundations

05 · PROJECT TYPES WE COVER

From 200 m of placa huella to 80 km of corridor.

  1. Placa huella · rural roadINVIAS tertiary roads manual. Test pits + CBR. Short timeline, low budget. Suited to rural municipalities or community contractors.
  2. Local urban streetInterlocking paver or flexible pavement. Coordination with water and sewer networks. Design + drainage + curbs + staking.
  3. Intermunicipal secondary roadCharacterization by homogeneous segments. Flexible pavement, longitudinal drainage, culverts, side slopes.
  4. Road concession / major corridorExtensive geotechnical campaign, geophysics, integrated hydraulics, final design, support during construction.
  5. Vehicular and pedestrian bridgesGeotechnics for piers and abutments, HEC-RAS hydraulics, HEC-18 scour analysis, construction recommendations.
  6. Tunnels and underground worksGeomechanical characterization of the rock mass, rock mass classification, ground support, monitoring.
