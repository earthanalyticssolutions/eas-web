02 · TYPICAL PROBLEMS

What clients in this industry bring to us.

  1. "I am going to build a warehouse"Soil investigation for long-span foundations, industrial pavement design, site drainage, on-site quality control. Coordination with the structural engineer.
  2. "My yard is sinking / cracking"Diagnosis of failed industrial pavement. Cause identification (subgrade, poor compaction, drainage, overloading). Rehabilitation design.
  3. "I need to expand the plant"Foundations for a new production line, verification of the existing ones, control of machinery vibrations, additional drainage.
  4. "I have heavy equipment that vibrates"Soil dynamic analysis, equipment foundation recommendations, seismic isolation, vibration monitoring.
  5. "My cold room floor is cracking"Forensic diagnosis accounting for thermal changes, freeze cycles, subgrade properties and concrete reinforcement.

05 · PROJECT TYPES WE COVER

From 500 m² to parks of 20+ ha.

  1. Small / medium warehouse (500-3,000 m²)Spread footings or slab foundation. Interlocking paver or concrete pavement. Basic drainage. Timeline 3-4 weeks.
  2. Long-span warehouse (3,000-15,000 m²)Deep foundations depending on load, reinforced industrial pavement, site drainage, expansion joints.
  3. Industrial plantSpecial equipment foundations, vibration control, industrial drainage, liquid handling according to environmental risk.
  4. Logistics center · 3PLLarge truck yards, reinforced maneuvering areas, loading/unloading docks, urban drainage system.
  5. Cold storage roomsThermally insulated subgrade, pavement adapted to freeze cycles, thermal joints. Specific analysis of cold floors.
  6. Free trade zone · industrial parkMaster planning with internal roads, utilities, stormwater drainage, individual lots with geotechnical site preparation.
06 · ANY PROJECT SIZE

One warehouse or an industrial park. Same operational demands.

In industry, a geotechnical failure means a stopped operation and multiplied costs. That is why we apply the same level of technical control to an 800 m² warehouse as to a 30,000 m² logistics center. The scope adapts to the complexity. The technical rigor does not.
