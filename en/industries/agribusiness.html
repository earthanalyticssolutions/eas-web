01 · SCOPE

For those who farm the land and need water.

Mid-size and large farmers, agribusinesses, irrigation districts, cattle ranches, forestry plantations. Your land, your water, your drainage are technical variables that EAS studies with the same rigor it applies to a civil works project.

Who we serve

  • Farmers (commercial crops)
  • Agribusiness (oil palm, sugarcane, banana, coffee)
  • Extensive and intensive livestock
  • Irrigation districts
  • Forestry plantations
  • Rural hotels / agritourism

Project types

  • Irrigation wells

  • Rural soil characterization
02 · TYPICAL PROBLEMS

What clients in this industry bring to us.

  1. "I need an irrigation well"Hydrogeological study, well design, pumping test, water quality and support for the ANA water concession.
  2. "My farm gets waterlogged / does not drain"Agricultural drainage study, identification of problem areas, design of channels and subsurface drains, management recommendations.
  3. "I want to prepare 50 ha for cultivation"Soil characterization, drone surveying, drainage, irrigation, land improvement drawings, budget.
  4. "I need a small dam / reservoir"Geotechnical study for the foundation, catchment hydrology, hydraulic design, storage capacity.
  5. "I bought a farm and want to know what I have"Comprehensive characterization: soils, water, risks, land-use suitability. Support for farming decisions.
03 · HOW WE SOLVE THEM

Three scopes. Your crop defines which one.

Household well · basic drainage INITIAL

  • Site visit + quick characterization
  • Basic well or drainage design
  • Timeline 2-3 weeks

Land improvement 20-100 ha INTERMEDIATE

  • Drone survey + soils
  • Drainage + irrigation design
  • Basic drawings · budget

Irrigation district · agribusiness ADVANCED

  • Full hydrogeological study
  • Hydraulic network design
  • ANA concession · ANLA support

Reservoir · farm pond (jagüey) SPECIALIZED

  • Foundation + watertightness
  • Catchment hydrology
  • Storage capacity

05 · PROJECT TYPES WE COVER

From 5 ha to 5,000 ha districts.

  1. Small / mid-size farmQuick characterization, household well, basic drainage, land-use recommendations. Short timeline, low budget.
  2. Commercial plantation (oil palm, sugarcane, banana)Land improvement, pressurized irrigation, agricultural drainage, integrated water management.
  3. Municipal irrigation districtDesign of main and secondary networks, intakes, hydraulic control, management plan.
  4. Agribusiness with a processing plantPlant foundations, industrial drainage, wastewater management, process water.
  5. Reservoir / small damFoundation, reservoir watertightness, capacity, spillway, safety criteria.
06 · ANY PROJECT SIZE

5 hectares or 5,000. Same technical rigor.

For a mid-size farmer, a poorly designed well means losing a season's crop. For agribusiness, a geotechnical failure means an operational shutdown. EAS applies the rigor your production needs, matching the scope to the real impact of the decision.

If it is small

  • Quick visit + recommendations
  • Basic functional design
  • Timeline of 1-2 weeks
  • Proportional pricing

If it is large

  • Coordinated team: hydrogeology + drainage + surveying
  • Full hydrogeological study
  • Hydraulic network design
  • ANA · ANLA · CAR support
  • Support during construction
