01 · SCOPE

Technical partners, not competitors.

Your client needs a soil investigation. Your project needs a geotechnical second opinion. Your structural engineer wants certainty about the subsurface. EAS is the technical partner that appears in your proposal and delivers under your seal, or stays behind the curtain with signed responsibility.

Who we support

  • Independent civil engineers
  • Architects with projects in development
  • Structural engineers
  • Technical oversight consultants
  • Builders who subcontract
  • Small and mid-size consulting firms
  • Urban architecture studios

Engagement models

  • Subcontracting of the geotechnical line
  • Signed technical second opinion
  • Technical opinion for the permitting office (curaduría)
  • Expert witness in technical arbitration
  • White-label (we deliver under your brand)
  • Geotechnical-structural coordination
02 · TYPICAL PROBLEMS

What other professionals bring to us.

  1. "My client needs a soil investigation · we subcontract"EAS does the study, you invoice as the integrator. Optional white-label. The end client stays yours.
  2. "I received an odd study and need a second opinion"Independent technical review of the existing study. Signed opinion with technical observations and recommendations.
  3. "I need the structural engineer to coordinate the foundation with the soil"Technical meetings with your structural engineer, parameter adjustment, validation of the proposed foundation.
  4. "I have a complex expert witness case"Technical support for arbitration, court cases or disputes between parties. Signed report with professional responsibility.
  5. "I need a technical opinion for the permitting office"Independent, defensible opinion, signed under a professional license.

05 · COLLABORATION TYPES

Professional relationship models.

  1. One-off subcontractingFor a specific project. EAS does the work, you integrate it. Full discretion: your client does not need to know we exist if you prefer it that way.
  2. Technical white-labelTechnical reports under your professional signature, with EAS behind the curtain under shared or silent professional liability.
  3. Framework agreementFor professionals with recurring work. Preferential rates, guaranteed timelines, a dedicated team.
  4. Interdisciplinary coordinationEAS as one more part of your team. Technical meetings, iterative adjustments, integration with structural design.
  5. Expert witness · legal supportIndependent technical opinion for arbitration, court cases or disputes. A defensible report.
