01 · SCOPE

For those who need the territory digitized.

Licensed cadastral operators, municipalities, departmental governments, IGAC, rural landowners with complex boundary issues, developers before permitting. The territory is measured and digitized, and legal, fiscal, environmental and construction decisions follow from it.

Who we serve

  • Licensed cadastral operators (OCH)
  • Municipalities and departmental governments
  • IGAC and territorial entities
  • Owners with complex boundary issues
  • Land developers and real estate developers
  • Builders (construction staking)
  • Environmental studies (mapping component)

Project types

  • Multipurpose cadastre (rural/urban)
  • Property survey
  • Boundary demarcation and monuments
  • Topographic surveys
  • Orthophoto and DTM
  • Official mapping
  • GIS analysis and geodatabase
02 · TYPICAL PROBLEMS

What clients in this industry bring to us.

  1. "I need a multipurpose cadastre for my municipality"Mass survey with RPAS drones, parcel identification, orthophoto, IGAC-compatible cadastral database.
  2. "I have a disputed boundary"Precision topographic survey with RTK, identification of existing monuments, official plan signed by a licensed surveyor.
  3. "I need an orthophoto and DTM of the area"Drone flight, photogrammetry, centimeter-level orthophoto, digital terrain model, deliverable geodatabase.
  4. "I am updating the land-use plan (POT / EOT)"Base mapping, GIS analysis, thematic maps, integration with the risk and environmental components.
  5. "I need construction staking"Staking with total station / GNSS RTK at millimeter-level precision, progress control.
03 · HOW WE SOLVE THEM

Three scopes. The area defines which one.

Construction staking ONGOING

05 · PROJECT TYPES WE COVER

From a single lot to entire municipalities.

  1. Single property surveyRural or urban lot, RTK + boundary monumentation, signed plan. For sale, inheritance, boundary demarcation, title deed.
  2. Rural district / urban neighborhoodDrone flight, orthophoto, parcel identification, cadastral database with owners.
  3. Municipal multipurpose cadastreCoverage of the entire municipality, IGAC integration, complete cadastral layers, support for land management.
  4. POT · EOT · reviewBase mapping, thematic layers, GIS analysis, final maps for adoption by decree.
  5. Ongoing construction stakingSurveying during construction, level control, geometry verification, support to the resident engineer.
06 · ANY PROJECT SIZE

One property or a whole municipality. Same accuracy.

A poorly measured property loses a sale. A poorly surveyed municipality loses tax revenue. EAS applies the same precise methodology whatever the area; only the team size and the field time change.

If it is small

  • Spot RTK · signed plan in days
  • Cost in proportion to the area
  • Support for a specific filing

If it is large

  • Coordinated team: drone + RTK + GIS
  • Flights by operating blocks
  • Deliverable QGIS/ArcGIS geodatabase
  • Technical support for IGAC / cadastral authorities
  • Signed official mapping
