01 · SCOPE

For those who turn a lot into a project.

Land developers, subdividers, boutique developers, country-community operators and master planners. Every lot has a geotechnical history, and we read it before construction discovers it the hard way.

Who we serve

  • Land developers and subdividers
  • Real estate developers
  • Operators of country and gated communities
  • Investors evaluating a lot
  • Master planners and urban design architects

Project types

  • Gated and open subdivisions
  • Rural parcel subdivisions
  • Suburban lot developments
  • Residential complexes
  • Large-scale master planning
02 · TYPICAL PROBLEMS

What clients in this industry bring to us.

  1. "I am buying a lot. Is it any good?"Geotechnical feasibility before purchase. Suitability for construction, risks, approximate foundation cost. The difference between a good deal and an expensive surprise.
  2. "I want to develop 5 ha"Comprehensive study: drone surveying, geotechnical feasibility, suitable vs critical zones, internal roads, site drainage, permit support.
  3. "My project is stuck at the permitting office"Technical support to address permitting office requirements, complete studies and issue feasibility opinions.
  4. "I have a country project with sloping areas"Slope stability analysis, critical zones, management recommendations, surface drainage, building restrictions.
  5. "The project has a wetland or water body nearby"Coordination with the environmental permit (CAR, regional environmental authority), flood studies, water management, regulatory setbacks.
03 · HOW WE SOLVE THEM

Three scopes. Your scale defines which one.

Small lot · quick feasibility INITIAL

  • Site visit + drone survey
  • Exploratory test pits
  • Signed feasibility opinion
  • Timeline 1-2 weeks

Mid-size subdivision INTERMEDIATE

  • SPT + full topographic survey
  • Critical zones identified
  • Drainage and internal roads
  • Basic site planning drawings

Master planning 50+ ha ADVANCED

  • Subsurface geophysics
  • Full geotechnical zoning
  • Urban drainage modeling
  • EIA + permitting office support

Lot pre-purchase DUE DILIGENCE

  • Visual inspection + document review
  • Identification of hidden risks
  • Foundation cost estimate
  • Technical opinion for the investor

05 · PROJECT TYPES WE COVER

From a single lot to 100+ ha.

  1. Single lot · pre-purchaseAn investor who wants to validate before buying. Inspection, document review, technical opinion. 5-7 days.
  2. Rural parcel subdivision 5-15 haDrone surveying, geotechnical feasibility, internal roads, drainage, suitability zoning. Typically 4-6 weeks.
  3. Mid-size subdivision 15-50 haComprehensive study: geotechnics, surveying, hydrology, risk, drainage, permit and permitting office support.
  4. Master planning 50+ haInterdisciplinary coordination, project phasing, primary infrastructure, urban modeling, EIA where applicable.
  5. Urban renewal / re-plattingPreviously developed lots with a change of use. Geotechnical re-evaluation accounting for man-made fills, existing foundations and utility connections.
06 · ANY PROJECT SIZE

One hectare or a hundred. Same technical level.

A small lot can hide as many risks as a large master plan. And a large master plan does not necessarily need the full advanced scope on every hectare. We match the technical depth to the real risk, not to the size of the checkbook.

If it is small

  • Quick visit + signed opinion
  • Timeline in days, not weeks
  • Focus on the critical risks of the lot
  • Price in proportion to size

If it is large

  • Coordinated team: geotechnics + surveying + hydrology + risk
  • Project phases · progressive deliverables
  • GIS modeling and progress dashboard
  • Support during construction

Evaluating, buying or developing?

Tell us about the lot and the phase. Three possible scopes depending on size and risk.
