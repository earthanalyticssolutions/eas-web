01 · SCOPE

For those who extract, transport or transform energy.

Open-pit and underground mines, hydropower plants, solar and wind farms, transmission lines, oil and gas pipelines. Operations where geotechnical risk means fines, operational shutdown or environmental liabilities. EAS provides in-depth characterization with a verifiable methodology.

Who we serve

  • Mining companies (coal, gold, emerald, construction materials)
  • Hydropower plants (large, small, micro)
  • Power transmission operators
  • Oil / gas pipeline operators

  • EPC contractors in the sector

Project types

  • Run-of-river or reservoir hydropower
  • Electrical substations
  • Transmission towers
  • Oil · gas · multiproduct pipelines
  • Large solar farms
  • Mine closure and restoration
02 · TYPICAL PROBLEMS

What clients in this industry bring to us.

  1. "I need to characterize the deposit without drilling so much"Surface geophysics (refraction seismic, electrical resistivity) to reduce the number of costly boreholes. Continuous subsurface imaging.
  2. "My mine slope is moving"Stability analysis, identification of the failure surface, design of remedial works, monitoring. Critical for operational safety.
  3. "I have a hydropower project"Dam foundation geotechnics, reservoir watertightness, rock mass hydrogeology, site seismic study.
  4. "I am closing the mine and need the plan"Restoration plan (PRMV), pit closure, acid drainage management, geomorphological restoration, ANM/ANLA support.
  5. "I need to characterize the subsurface along my transmission line"Tower foundations in diverse conditions (soft soil, rock, slopes), access roads, slopes at tower sites.

05 · PROJECT TYPES WE COVER

From a quarry to a 500 MW hydropower plant.

  1. Quarries and aggregate minesGeomechanical characterization, cut slope stability, operating plan, closure plan. Support for mid-size operators.
  2. Metal mining · open pitOre characterization, mine slopes, drainage, geotechnical monitoring plans, liability management.
  3. Underground miningRock mass geomechanical characterization, ground support, geotechnical ventilation, roof and wall monitoring.
  4. Run-of-river hydropowerGeotechnics of intake works and powerhouse, hydrogeology, headrace tunnel stability.
  5. Reservoir hydropowerDam foundation, reservoir hydrogeology (watertightness), site seismic analysis.
  6. Transmission lines and substationsTower foundations in diverse soils, access roads, stabilization of critical sites.
  7. Oil and gas pipelinesRoute geotechnics, river crossings, right-of-way stabilization, operational geotechnical monitoring.
06 · ANY PROJECT SIZE

A 5 ha quarry or a 500 ha mine. Same technical depth.

In the extractive and energy sector, geotechnical risks are not proportional to size. A mid-size quarry can have a critical slope as risky as one in a major mine. EAS applies the rigor your operation actually needs, whatever the production volume.

If it is small

  • Technical reconnaissance + recommendations
  • Basic geotechnical operating plan
  • Support for small mining titles
  • Proportional pricing

If it is large

  • Coordinated team: geophysics + hydrogeology + geotechnics
  • Advanced numerical modeling
  • Permanent monitoring plan
  • Support for EIA/PMA filings with ANLA
  • Coordination with the oversight consultant
